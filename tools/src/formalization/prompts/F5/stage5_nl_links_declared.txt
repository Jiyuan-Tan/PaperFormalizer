The items below are the paper's assumptions and definitions, not results: read "result" as "assumption or definition". An item's segments are the text that states it, and its rows are those of the Lean declaration that formalizes it, in reading order: its parameters and whatever quantifiers or hypotheses its body opens with, (a `def`) the row of its name applied to its parameters — it may stand after those opening rows —, and the clauses that define it, cut as a conclusion is; a record gives one clause per field (`name : …`). The row of the name takes the segments that name or introduce the notion; a parameter row, the segments that introduce that object.
